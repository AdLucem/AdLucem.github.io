TESTING

The way marginalized people are shown in media, including by Large Language Models (LLMs), greatly influences how they are seen in society. Even if LLM outputs don't seem openly negative, the hidden ideas and associations they hold can lead to problems. For instance, an LLM acting as a therapist might try to encourage a disabled patient by referring to "inspiration porn" (stories of high-achieving disabled people), which could actually be harmful. The paper highlights a pattern where LLMs often focus too much on the gender or sexuality of queer individuals, even when it's not relevant to the conversation. In contrast, non-LGBTQ+ individuals are shown with a wide variety of roles and experiences. This study aims to describe how LLMs create narrow, harmful, and "othering" representations of queer people, treating them as distinct from the majority.
